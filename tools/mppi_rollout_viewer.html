Color encodes each sample's rank among this batch, not its raw cost — a few guardrail-violating samples can carry costs orders of magnitude above the rest, which would otherwise crush every other path to one color. The green path is the single lowest-cost sample; the blue marker/arrow is the ego vehicle; the red marker is an obstacle (sized to its real radius in World shape) — once it leaves the sensor's forward view (e.g. after the car passes it), it dims to show its last known position rather than vanishing. The shaded ribbon is the drivable lane surface between the current left/right limits; the dashed line down its middle is lane center. World shape plots real ground position (matches the driving screen, curves and all); road-relative plots MPPI's own internal frame (distance along the road vs. lateral offset), where the road is always a flat band — useful for reading lateral error directly, but it won't look like the real path on a curve. Frames were captured every 0.5s while MPPI was actively engaged, not the whole run.
